# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

SubLedger is built so that your information never leaves your phone. This page explains exactly what that means.

## What SubLedger collects

Nothing. SubLedger has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The author does not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

## What stays on your phone

The subscriptions you type in (names, prices, dates, categories, notes) and your settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.

## No internet access

The app does not request the internet permission, or any other permission. Android will not let an app without that permission open a network connection, so SubLedger cannot upload, sync or share your data even by accident. You can check this yourself in the app info screen on your phone, or in the file app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml in this repository.

## Backups you create

If you choose Export backup, the app asks Android to save a file where you pick. From that moment the file is yours and sits wherever you put it, including any cloud folder you choose. If you choose Import backup, the app reads only the one file you select. Automatic Android and Google backup is switched off for this app on purpose, so your data is not copied to any cloud unless you export a file yourself.

## Deleting your data

Use Delete all data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Either one removes everything the app stored. Backup files you exported are separate files and you need to delete those yourself.

## Children

SubLedger collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

SubLedger contains no third party code, libraries, SDKs or services. It runs on the Android system WebView that comes with your phone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new effective date, and the change history is visible in the commit log.
